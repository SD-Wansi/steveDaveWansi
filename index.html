About Me

Biography

Steve Dave Wansi is a postdoctoral researcher at CEMES (UPR 8011) - CNRS, Toulouse since November 2025, specializing in atomistic modeling of amorphous materials, nanoparticles, and interfaces. Their work combines Density Functional Theory (DFT), Molecular Dynamics (MD), and machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIP) that they have developed. Steve Dave Wansi earned a PhD in Computational Physics from the University of Strasbourg, focusing on computational modeling of complex materials systems. Their research interests include the theoretical and computational study of material properties at the atomic scale, bridging physics-based simulations with machine learning techniques to design and understand novel materials.
